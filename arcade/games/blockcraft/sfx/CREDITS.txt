BlockCraft sound effects - credits
==================================

Every WAV file in this folder is a short clip cut from a free sound pack, then trimmed, faded, levelled
and converted to mono 16-bit WAV for the game. None of it comes from another game.

Licences

             Credit required: the pig sounds (pig_*.wav) are from "Pig SFX Pack" by Vinrax,
             https://opengameart.org/content/pig-sfx-pack - changed here (trimmed, faded, levelled, WAV).

The music and the fallback sounds are synthesised by the game's own code (sounds.py).
